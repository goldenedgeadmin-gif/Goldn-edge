💬 Help & Support

Find quick answers below, or chat with our support team on WhatsApp for anything else.

📦 How do I track my order? Go to My Orders and open the order you'd like to track.
💳 What payment methods are accepted? We accept secure card and bank transfer payments in Naira at checkout, powered by Paystack.
🚚 How long does delivery take? Most orders arrive within 24–48 hours depending on your location. Same-day delivery is available in select areas.
↩️ Can I return an item? Yes — items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
📱 Do you sell only phones? No — we also sell phone accessories, small gadgets, fashion accessories and wears, all under Golden Edge quality checks.
Still need help? Tap the WhatsApp button in the corner to chat with our support team directly.
