Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Golden Edge collects when you use this site, what it's used for, and who it's shared with. We've kept it in plain language rather than dense legal text.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google — your name, email address and profile picture, so we can identify your account, greet you by name, and show you your own orders and cart.

When you check out — your delivery name, phone number, WhatsApp number, address, city, state and country, so we can fulfil and deliver your order.

Payment details — handled entirely by Paystack, our payment processor. We never see or store your card details ourselves; Paystack processes the transaction and tells us only whether it succeeded.

How we use it

To create and manage your account, process and deliver your orders, let you track order status, and respond when you contact us (including via WhatsApp).

We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your account, order and cart information is stored in a secure database hosted by Neon (neon.tech). Sign-in itself is handled by Google — we never see or store your Google password.

Your choices

You can browse the shop without signing in at all — an account is only needed to check out, track orders, or use the cart.

To ask us to update or delete your information, contact us via the WhatsApp button on the site or through the Help page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us via WhatsApp (the chat button in the corner) or the Help page.
